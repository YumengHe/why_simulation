Overview

Why simulation? It lets us try an action in a modeled world, calculate what happens, and repeat the experiment with different conditions.

This free online book follows one familiar task: opening a drawer. We begin with how AI models understand instructions, then build the 3D object and describe how it moves. Simple explanations, illustrations, and moving examples connect these ideas.

  1. Text-only LLM → VLM → VLA — from language to images and robot actions.
  2. 3D generation — creating objects and choosing how to represent them.
  3. Articulated objects — parts, joints, URDF files, and simulated motion.
